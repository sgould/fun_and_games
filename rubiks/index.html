Rubik's Cube Scanner & Solver

Show each face to the webcam, check the colours, then watch the solution.

1 · Scan

Waiting for camera permission…

Hold the cube so a face fills the grid, pause for a moment, then turn the whole cube to show another face, in any order and any way up. Don't twist the layers. The grid turns yellow while it is reading and flashes when it takes a face. Showing faces more than once makes the reading more reliable.

2 · Check

Click a colour, then click any sticker that was read wrongly. Centres are fixed.

Preparing solver…

3 · Solve

Drag the cube to look around. Click a move to jump to it. Notation: a letter alone is a clockwise quarter turn, ' is anticlockwise, 2 is a half turn.
